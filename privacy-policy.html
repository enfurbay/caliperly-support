On your device.

This policy explains how Caliperly, developed by Enes Furkan Bayram, handles information in its iPhone and iPad app and on this support website. Contact: enfurbay@gmail.com.

Information in the app

Caliperly does not require an account and does not send your ECG images, measurements, notes or study files to the developer. Measurement and calculation processing takes place on your device. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Local storage and deletion

Imported files, measurements, annotations, confirmations and settings are stored locally. Study files use iOS file protection and are excluded from automatic device backups. You can delete studies in the app. Deleting the app removes its local studies; exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them. We do not operate a server that can recover deleted studies.

Imports, exports and sharing

Caliperly accesses files or photos you choose through system interfaces. Exporting or sharing transfers the selected content only to a destination you choose. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Reports can contain identifying information, so review them before sharing. Image redactions are flattened into PDF and annotated-image exports, but titles, notes and text annotations are not automatically anonymized. Editable study backups include original source data and editable redactions.

Permissions and reminders

Notification permission is requested only if you enable an optional weekly reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally and do not include patient details. You can turn them off in the app or iOS Settings. The app does not request tracking permission.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, ratings and any diagnostics you choose to share through Apple's systems under its own privacy practices. Caliperly does not receive your payment-card details.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and address the issue, retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations, and do not use it for advertising. Please do not send patient data. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website and external links

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email services and websites you open through external links handle information under their own policies.

Children and sensitive data

Caliperly is designed for trained users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. Users are responsible for protecting the sensitive information contained in files they import or share.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when privacy practices change and revise the date above. For privacy questions or requests about information you voluntarily sent to support, contact enfurbay@gmail.com.
