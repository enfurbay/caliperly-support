CALIPERLY HELP

A clearer workflow.

Questions about Caliperly? Email enfurbay@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce the issue. Please use synthetic examples and do not send patient records or identifying health information.

Start a measurement

  1. Import an image or an unlocked PDF from Photos or Files, or try the generated practice ECG.
  2. Check that the trace is clear and grid spacing is uniform. Crop one consistent region if necessary. Avoid distorted photographs.
  3. Calibrate the time axis using a known span. Add a second reference in a separate location. Calibrate voltage independently when needed.
  4. Place the measurement endpoints, zoom in and adjust carefully. Confirm that the selected paper speed and gain match your source.
  5. Review the source and results before confirming a measurement or exporting a report.

Why is confirmation unavailable?

Complete the source check and valid calibration first. Time and voltage confirmation require two references agreeing within 2%, with each spanning at least 40 source pixels. This is an engineering consistency check, not a clinical accuracy guarantee. Paired QT results also require matching lead and beat context. Relevant edits change the status to “Review required.”

Why do QTc methods differ?

Bazett, Fridericia, Framingham and Hodges use different correction formulas. Review the measured QT, mean RR, selected method and clinical context. The calculator does not classify risk or provide a diagnosis. QT at or above mean RR is not supported.

Import limits

Choose images of 32 megapixels or fewer and files below 40 MB. PDFs must be unlocked and contain no more than 20 pages. Split larger PDFs before importing.

Back up before deleting

Studies are stored locally and excluded from automatic device backups. Export editable study backups to preserve your work. Deleting a study or the app removes local data. We cannot retrieve it from a server.

Share carefully

PDF and annotated-image exports flatten image redactions. Titles, notes and text annotations need a separate review. Editable backups retain original source data and are not anonymized. Choose recipients and destinations carefully.

Notifications and purchases

Weekly reminders are optional and can be disabled in Settings. Caliperly is planned as a one-time purchase with no subscription. App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

Important use information

Caliperly is a manual measurement aid for trained users, not an ECG recorder, diagnostic service or emergency service. Source quality, calibration and endpoint placement affect results. Do not rely on it as the sole basis for clinical decisions. Clinical validation and release review are pending.
